# Screenplays

The one family where everything is marked, which makes almost every judgement a reading rather
than an inference.

- **A slug line is a transition and a setting at once.** `INT. VANCE'S LAB - NIGHT` marks the cut,
  names the place, and states the time of day. Treat it as a marked boundary — it is never
  suppressed by a length floor, and a one-line scene after one is a real scene.
- **`CUT TO:`, `FADE IN:`, `DISSOLVE TO:` are transitions.**
- **A character cue above dialogue is the speaker**, exactly as written. It resolves with no
  inference.
- **Action lines are `action`; parentheticals belong to the speech they qualify.**
- **Mode is `Enacted` almost throughout.** Voice-over is `disembodied` speech addressed to the
  audience, which makes it `Narrated`.
- Front matter is a title page and a `FADE IN:`; there is rarely any back matter.
